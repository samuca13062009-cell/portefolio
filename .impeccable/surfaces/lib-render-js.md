# Portefólio público (todas as páginas)

Scope: the public site (Home, Sobre, Projetos, Projeto, CV, Contacto, legal pages). Visitor mode: Experience. The backoffice is out of scope.

Audience: recruiters, teachers and freelance clients, equally. They must leave believing he builds real things that work and is reliable at work. Proof is only real content from data/content.json and real screenshots. Constraints: four themes and dark mode stay; light and fast on phones; never childish; strict CSP, self-hosted fonts; WCAG AA.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A serious, human portfolio where the real work leads: actual screenshots of his projects, his own words, his real job. The owner rejected the datasheet/chip world (seed 77fda9ab) as cartoonish and took the category standard; it is executed straight, at full craft, with no illustrative costume.

OWN-WORLD: Plain theme ground, no texture. Strong display type for headings, calm body text. Real screenshots in rounded frames with a soft offset shadow carry all the colour. Quiet lists and hairline rows for experience and skills. One dark band for the closing call to action.

STORY: The visitor reads who he is, sees two real projects at large size, reads the human paragraph about working at a restaurant alongside his experience, scans skills, and contacts him or downloads the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Greeting headline left at display size, role line, lead, primary "Ver projetos" and secondary "Descarregar CV"; a facts card on the right (or his photo once provided). The first real screenshot starts just below.

FORM: The category standard (owner's choice after rejecting candidate 3 of seed 77fda9ab), executed at full craft.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
